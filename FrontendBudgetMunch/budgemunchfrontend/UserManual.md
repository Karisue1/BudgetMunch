# User Manual


## Introduction
Budget Munch is designed to help users find local restaurants within their budget. With features like filtering options and password recovery, the app makes dining out affordable, secure, and convenient.

## Features Overview
* Budget Setting: Enter your budget to filter dining options.
* Address-Based Search: Discover nearby restaurants by inputting your location.
* Filters: Refine results by price, name, vicinity, or rating.
* Search Bar: Quickly locate specific restaurants on table.
* Account Security: Reset forgotten passwords using email tokens for confirmation.
* Profile Page: Profile page with the current logged in user's information


## How to Use the Application

### 1. About Us
* Get familiarized with BudgetMunch through our slide show on the About Us page
* Click the Get Started Toggle when you scroll down to either login or create a new account if you don't already have one.

### 2. Registration
* Click the login button on the navigation bar or on the Get Started toggle foun.
* To create new account click on the "Register now here" link
* Enter your name, email, username, and password.
* Click Register to create your account.
### 3. Logging In
* On the Login Page.
* Enter your registered email and password.
* Click Login to access your account.
### 4. Finding Restaurants
* Input your street address, city, state, and budget into the input boxes.
* Click Submit to generate a table that contains a list of restaurants nearby that are within your budget or click the clear button to clear all fields.
* Click on the restaurant name hyperlinks that will take you to a google search to learn more about the restaurant.
* Use the search bar to filter out and search for information on the table
* Toggle the arrow buttons on the column names to sort the data in the table from ascending to descending order and vice versa.

### 5. Sidebar Menu
* The side bar menu contains routes to the Home page (where the restaurant table is), About Us page, Profile page, and a Logout button that will take you back to the About Us Page.

### 6. Profile Page
* When you click on the profile page you will be able to see the your name, email, and username

### 7. LogOut
* You can simply sign out of your account by clickling the logout toggle on the sidebar or logout button on the navigation bar to exit your current screen and go back to the About Us Page

### 8. Password Recovery
* Select the Forgot Password link on the login page.
* Enter the email you used to register, in order to receive a reset token.
* Submit the token from your email onto the input box 
* Input your new password and reset it
* Now you will be able to login with your same username and new password

## Troubleshooting
### Common Issues:
#### Restaurant Search Returns No Results:
* Ensure the address is correct and budget is greater than zero.

#### Table Search Returns No Results:
* Ensure that what you are inputting in the search bar is existing data in the table.

#### Password Recovery Fails:
* Make sure you are correctly inputting the email you registered with 
* Check your spam folder for recovery emails.
* Ensure the recovery code is entered accurately.
